# SPINOFIT website

Static, responsive profile website for SPINOFIT, a physiotherapy brand intended to serve Brisbane, Queensland. The design takes inspiration from the clean white personal-portfolio layout of Reeni, adapted for a healthcare profile and general health education.

## Structure
- `index.html` — home/profile, service overview, FAQs and enquiry form
- `articles/index.html` — articles hub
- `articles/*.html` — three educational articles
- `assets/css/styles.css` — responsive design system
- `assets/js/main.js` — mobile navigation and footer year
- `privacy.html` — draft privacy notice requiring business-owner review
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` — search engine discovery

## Preview / deployment
This is a static site and can be hosted with GitHub Pages, Netlify or another static host. If deploying to Netlify, confirm that the site is connected and that Netlify Forms is enabled for the `spinofit-enquiry` form. The current form will not collect submissions on plain GitHub Pages without a separate form backend. Test an enquiry end-to-end before launch.

## Launch checklist — required
1. Replace the portrait placeholder with the supplied, approved physiotherapist photo. Do not publish the illustrated placeholder as the real person.
2. Confirm the practitioner’s full name, Australian registration details, qualifications, exact experience, scope of practice and the services offered.
3. Confirm the spelling of ANcara and whether the practitioner/business has permission to reference this organisation in public marketing.
4. Confirm the exact Brisbane clinic address or actual service area, booking method, fees and availability. Avoid advertising unconfirmed services or locations.
5. Verify the email address `hello@spinofit.com.au`, or replace it throughout the website with the active business email.
6. Configure and test the enquiry form backend. GitHub Pages does not process HTML form submissions by itself.
7. Finalise the privacy notice for the actual business entity, form processor, storage, retention and privacy obligations.
8. Have all health information, articles, service descriptions and advertising reviewed by the responsible registered practitioner. Australian regulated-health-service advertising must not include prohibited testimonials or misleading claims.
9. Add analytics and Google Search Console only with appropriate privacy notices and consent/controls where required. Verify the sitemap after the custom domain resolves.
10. Confirm domain DNS, HTTPS, mobile rendering, keyboard access, form validation and error states before launch.

## SEO foundations included
- Australian English, page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs and social-sharing metadata
- Physiotherapy / Brisbane topical wording used naturally
- Physiotherapy business structured data (review details, especially address and business representation, before going live)
- Sitemap, robots rules, article metadata and internal linking

SEO cannot guarantee rankings. Local discoverability will also depend on accurate business details, the real clinic/service area, practitioner credibility, useful content, technical performance and a complete Google Business Profile if eligible.

## Health-content note
The article pages are general educational drafts, not individual diagnosis or treatment advice. The responsible practitioner should review them before publication. Avoid publishing patient testimonials or promised outcomes in regulated-health-service advertising; check current Ahpra guidance.
